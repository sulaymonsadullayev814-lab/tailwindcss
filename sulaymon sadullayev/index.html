About Me

I'm a frontend developer who obsesses over the details that most people never notice — the 60fps scroll, the animation curve that feels just right, the component that works beautifully on a 320px screen and a 4K monitor alike.

I started my journey writing janky jQuery and now I spend my days building scalable React architectures and obsessing over Lighthouse scores. I believe the web should be fast, accessible, and beautiful — in that order.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me writing about frontend patterns, contributing to open source, or exploring the latest browser APIs that most developers won't use for another two years.
